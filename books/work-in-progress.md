<h2 class="mybooks">Professional Experience and Activities</h2>

//Scribe’s Anthology Project// – An annual project where more writers are given the opportunity to get published. Writers are offered the chance to put a short story through two thorough workshops and receive in-depth critiques from experienced writers. At the end of the process, the stories are put before a panel of judges and the successful stories are sent to publishers as an anthology, 2004 to 2006

//AFWD Short Story Competition// – two categories (unpublished and published). The members of A Fantasy Writers Dream Writers Group wrote short stories which were judged by successful Australian authors Jennifer Fallon and Fiona McIntosh, 2003

//Scribe’s Message Board// – A message board for writers from around the world. Most discussions are to do with the craft of writing, in depth crits are also offered in a private forum, February 2003 to 31 August 2009

//The Desk of Karen Lee Field// (formerly known as Scribe’s Writing Desk) – A website for writers with writing tips and resources, April 2002 to present
