100 STORIES FOR QUEENSLAND has something for everyone, from slice of life to science fiction, fantasy to romance, paranormal to literary fiction. Heart-warming, quirky, inspiring and funny the stories between these covers will lift readers to higher ground.

100 Stories for Queensland is a charity anthology to assist the victims of the Queensland floods. Stories are donated by writers from across the globe and includes my story “Amunet's Gift”.

100% of the profit from the sale of the anthology will be donated to the Queensland Premier’s Flood Relief Appeal.
